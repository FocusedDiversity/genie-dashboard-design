Burnout Prevention Recommendations

  1. Cap New Patient Visits at 4 per provider per day to allow adequate documentation time.
  2. Flag and review scheduling for providers exceeding the cap, especially chronic cases.
  3. Monitor the monthly trend — rising high-NPV days may signal a staffing or redistribution need.
  4. Run provider feedback surveys for chronically overloaded providers to assess real burnout symptoms.

NPV load also correlates with longer documentation turnaround time (Doc TAT) on high-volume days.
